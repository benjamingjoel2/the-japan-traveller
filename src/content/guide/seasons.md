Japan runs about 3,000 km from north to south, so each season arrives at a different time in each region. Use the wheel above to open any month and see what is happening region by region, and which verified operators run trips that month.

## Cherry blossom (sakura)

The blossom front moves from south to north. Okinawa's early-flowering cherry opens in January and February. On the main islands the season usually starts in Kyushu and Shikoku in late March, reaches Tokyo and Kyoto in late March to early April, moves through the Alps and Tohoku in mid to late April, and reaches Hokkaido from late April into May.

The Japan Meteorological Agency's normal (1991–2020 average) first-flowering date for Tokyo is 24 March. The Japan Weather Association lists Kyoto's normal flowering around 26 March and full bloom around 4 April. Real dates move from year to year; forecasts are published from late winter. See [When is the best week for cherry blossom in Kyoto?](/questions/best-week-for-cherry-blossom-in-kyoto)

## Rainy season (tsuyu)

The rainy season reaches Okinawa first, usually starting in May and ending in late June. From Kyushu to Kanto it usually runs from early June to mid or late July, and in Tohoku from mid June. Hokkaido has no formal rainy season. It does not rain every day. See [Is Japan good in the rainy season?](/questions/is-japan-good-in-the-rainy-season)

## Summer heat

After the rainy season ends, July and August are hot and humid across most of Honshu, Shikoku and Kyushu. This is the season of the big summer festivals and of hiking in the Alps and Hokkaido. Typhoons are most frequent from August to September, and Okinawa and western Japan are most exposed. Mid-August (Obon) is a peak domestic travel period.

## Autumn leaves (koyo)

Autumn colour moves in the opposite direction to the blossom: from the high mountains and Hokkaido in late September and October, through Tohoku and the Alps in October, to Tokyo, Kyoto and Kyushu from mid November into early December.

## Winter snow

Hokkaido, the Sea of Japan side of Tohoku and the Northern Alps receive heavy snow from December to March, which is the ski season (Niseko, Furano, Hakuba, Nozawa Onsen). Winters on the Pacific side (Tokyo, Osaka) are mostly dry and clear. Shirakawa-go is at its most photographed under snow in January and February.

## Busy holiday periods

Domestic travel peaks at New Year (roughly 29 December to 3 January), Golden Week (29 April to 5 May) and Obon (mid August). Trains and inns fill early at these times.

Rules change. Check the Ministry of Foreign Affairs page for your nationality before you book, and again before you fly.

## Visa exemption

Japan has visa exemption arrangements for short-term stays with 74 countries and regions (Ministry of Foreign Affairs list, page dated 1 September 2025). Short-term stay covers tourism, visiting friends and business meetings; it does not allow paid work.

- For most exempt countries the period of stay granted on arrival is **90 days**. This includes the United States, the United Kingdom, the EU member states and Brazil.
- Brazilian nationals with ordinary passports have been visa-exempt for stays of up to 90 days since **30 September 2023**.
- A few countries have shorter periods (for example 15 days for Indonesia and Thailand, which also have registration requirements).
- Travellers from countries not on the list need a visa from a Japanese embassy or consulate.

## Passport

Your passport must be valid for the length of your stay. Check the Ministry of Foreign Affairs page and your airline's rules for any additional requirement.

## Visit Japan Web

Visit Japan Web is the government's online service for immigration and customs declarations. Registering before you fly gives you QR codes to show on arrival. It is optional; paper forms are still available.

## Departure tax

The International Tourist Tax is charged on every departure from Japan by air or sea, usually included in the ticket price. It rose from ¥1,000 to **¥3,000 per departure on 1 July 2026**. Tickets issued on or before 30 June 2026 were charged the old rate. Children under two are exempt.

## Arrival

Most international flights arrive at Narita or Haneda (Tokyo) or Kansai (Osaka). Rail links into the cities are in the [rail-route table](/rail-routes).

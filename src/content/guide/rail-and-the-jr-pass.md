## How the network fits together

Japan's long-distance rail is run by six regional JR companies plus many private railways. The Shinkansen (high-speed) lines form a spine from Kagoshima in Kyushu to Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto in Hokkaido, with branches to Kanazawa and Tsuruga (Hokuriku), Niigata, Akita and Yamagata. Regional limited express trains connect the Shinkansen to places such as Takayama, the Kii Peninsula and Shikoku.

Private railways are not part of JR. The ones travellers meet most are Odakyu (Shinjuku to Hakone), Tobu (Asakusa to Nikko), Kintetsu (Kyoto and Osaka to Nara and Ise) and Nankai (Osaka to Koyasan and Kansai Airport).

## The Japan Rail Pass

The nationwide Japan Rail Pass gives unlimited travel on most JR trains for 7, 14 or 21 consecutive days. Prices rose sharply in October 2023. Bought online from the official site, the current prices are:

| Pass | Ordinary, adult | Ordinary, child | Green, adult | Green, child |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 days | ¥50,000 | ¥25,000 | ¥70,000 | ¥35,000 |
| 14 days | ¥80,000 | ¥40,000 | ¥110,000 | ¥55,000 |
| 21 days | ¥100,000 | ¥50,000 | ¥140,000 | ¥70,000 |

Bought from an overseas JR-designated agency, each pass costs more (for example ¥53,000 for a 7-day ordinary adult pass). Children are aged 6 to 11. Green is first class.

The fastest trains on the Tokaido, Sanyo and Kyushu Shinkansen, Nozomi and Mizuho, can be used with the pass only if you buy a separate special ticket for each journey. Hikari, Sakura and Kodama trains on the same lines are covered without it.

The pass is not valid on private railways, most city subways, or buses run by non-JR companies.

## Is it worth it?

It depends on the route. For a classic Tokyo, Kyoto and back trip it usually is not; for a long route that covers several regions in one week it may be. Price your journeys individually first and compare. See [Is the JR Pass still worth it?](/questions/is-the-jr-pass-still-worth-it) Regional JR passes, sold by each JR company, are often better value for trips that stay in one area.

## Booking seats and luggage

Reserved seats on the Shinkansen can be booked online (for the Tokaido, Sanyo and Kyushu lines through SmartEX) or at stations. Large suitcases (161 to 250 cm in total dimensions) on the Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and Nishi-Kyushu Shinkansen need a seat with an oversized baggage area reserved in advance; see [sending your luggage ahead](/articles/luggage-forwarding-takkyubin). Many travellers instead send bags ahead by courier (takkyubin) to their next hotel.

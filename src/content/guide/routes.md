These are the routes most first-time visitors take, written as a starting point. Nights are a suggestion; travel times come from the [rail-route table](/rail-routes). Each route starts in Tokyo; reverse it if you fly into Osaka (Kansai).

## 7 days: Tokyo and Kyoto

| Nights | Base | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Tokyo | Arrive Narita (N'EX, about 1:00) or Haneda |
| 4 | Kyoto | Tokaido Shinkansen, about 2:15 (Nozomi) |

Day trips from Kyoto: Nara (JR, about 0:45) and Osaka. Fly home from Kansai (Haruka, about 1:15 from Kyoto) to avoid returning to Tokyo.

Rail pass: not worth it. A one-way Tokyo to Kyoto ticket plus local fares costs well under a 7-day pass.

## 10 days: adding Hakone and Hiroshima

| Nights | Base | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Tokyo | Arrive Narita or Haneda |
| 1 | Hakone | Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku, about 1:25 (not JR) |
| 4 | Kyoto | Odawara to Kyoto by Shinkansen |
| 1 | Hiroshima | Shinkansen via Shin-Osaka; Miyajima as a side trip |
| 1 | Osaka | Shinkansen back to Shin-Osaka, fly home from Kansai |

Rail pass: usually still not worth it for this route; price it both ways.

## 14 days: the Alps and the Japan Sea coast

| Nights | Base | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Tokyo | Arrive Narita or Haneda |
| 2 | Kanazawa | Hokuriku Shinkansen, about 2:30 |
| 2 | Takayama | Bus via Shirakawa-go (not JR) |
| 4 | Kyoto | Hida limited express to Nagoya, then Shinkansen |
| 1 | Hiroshima | Shinkansen via Shin-Osaka |
| 2 | Osaka | Shinkansen back; fly home from Kansai |

Rail pass: a 14-day pass can come close to paying for itself on this route, depending on day trips. Add up the individual fares before you buy.

## Variations

- **Walking:** replace Takayama with two or three days on the Nakasendo (Magome to Tsumago) or the Kumano Kodo (from Kii-Tanabe, about 2:15 from Shin-Osaka).
- **Winter:** replace Kanazawa and Takayama with Hokkaido (fly from Tokyo) or the Northern Alps for snow.
- **Shikoku and the art islands:** from Okayama, the Marine Liner reaches Takamatsu in about 0:55; ferries go on to Naoshima.

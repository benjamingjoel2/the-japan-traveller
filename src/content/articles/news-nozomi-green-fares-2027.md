On 16 October 2026 JR Central and JR West announced that reserved seats on Nozomi and Mizuho trains on the Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen will cost more for tickets bought from 1 July 2027. Green Car (first class) charges on all trains on the two lines will rise for journeys from 15 December 2027. The companies say inflation has pushed up the cost of maintaining safety and service. Unreserved seats on Nozomi and Mizuho keep their current price.

## What changes and when

There are two separate changes with two different start dates.

- **Reserved ordinary seats on Nozomi and Mizuho:** the new reserved-seat limited express charge applies to tickets **purchased** from 1 July 2027. A ticket bought before that date for a later journey is sold at the current price.
- **Green Cars on all Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen trains:** the new Green charge applies to journeys **made** from 15 December 2027.
- **Unreserved seats:** the limited express charge for non-reserved seats on Nozomi and Mizuho is not being revised.

The changes also apply to the Tokaido and Sanyo part of a journey that continues onto the Kyushu Shinkansen. According to The Japan News, this is the first rise on the Tokaido section since the Nozomi was introduced in March 1992, apart from consumption-tax changes.

## Example prices

The companies give these total prices (fare plus charge) for one adult, one way, in the regular season, using a Nozomi or Mizuho for the whole trip.

| Route | Reserved seat now | From 1 July 2027 | Green Car now | From 15 Dec 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tokyo to Nagoya | ¥11,300 | ¥11,880 | ¥14,960 | ¥18,050 |
| Tokyo to Shin-Osaka | ¥14,720 | ¥15,420 | ¥19,590 | ¥23,290 |
| Tokyo to Okayama | ¥17,770 | ¥18,530 | ¥23,840 | ¥28,400 |
| Tokyo to Hiroshima | ¥19,760 | ¥20,620 | ¥27,020 | ¥32,390 |
| Shin-Osaka to Hiroshima | ¥10,950 | ¥11,210 | ¥14,610 | ¥17,380 |
| Shin-Osaka to Hakata | ¥16,020 | ¥16,300 | ¥22,090 | ¥26,170 |

The Green charge itself is set by distance and rises more steeply. For up to 100 km it goes from ¥1,300 to ¥2,100. For 801 km or more it goes from ¥7,790 to ¥12,300. Adults and children pay the same Green charge.

Online prices through the EX services (Express Reservation, smartEX and EX from LINE) rise by the same amounts. A standard smartEX reserved seat from Tokyo to Shin-Osaka goes from ¥14,520 to ¥15,220. The EX notice says early-booking discounts will also be revised, with details to follow. The private Supreme Class compartments, which began running in October 2026, are revised on the same dates.

## Green Car improvements

The companies list Green Car improvements on N700A and N700S trains. From 15 December 2027 they will be rolled out in stages:

- two seat types in the Green Cars, one with a partition between neighbours;
- a smaller recline angle (from 123 to 116 degrees) with a fold-up footrest for more legroom;
- waiting rooms inside the ticket gates for Green Car and Supreme Class passengers. They open at Shinagawa, Shin-Yokohama, Kyoto, Kokura and Hakata on 15 December 2027, at Tokyo and Shin-Osaka in fiscal 2028, and at Nagoya in fiscal 2029. Hiroshima will follow in or after fiscal 2028, with no date set yet.

Free Wi-Fi is being extended to more Green Cars on N700S trains in stages from 26 October 2026.

## What it means for travellers

**Japan Rail Pass holders.** The Japan Rail Pass does not cover Nozomi or Mizuho seats without a separate special ticket. On the pass site, that ticket costs ¥4,960 from Tokyo to Shin-Osaka, as of 7 November 2026. The 16 October announcement does not mention the Japan Rail Pass or the special ticket. The pass site does not say whether the special ticket price will change. See [rail and the JR Pass](/guide/rail-and-the-jr-pass) and [is the JR Pass still worth it?](/questions/is-the-jr-pass-still-worth-it).

**Timing your booking.**

- If you travel between July and mid-December 2027 and buy a reserved Nozomi ticket before 1 July, you pay the current reserved-seat price.
- Green Car prices depend on the travel date, not the purchase date, so early booking does not avoid the December 2027 rise.
- Unreserved seats remain an option at the current price. All Nozomi seats are reserved during the main holiday peaks; see [do I need to reserve Shinkansen seats?](/questions/do-i-need-to-reserve-shinkansen-seats)

**What we don't know yet.**

- New prices for EX early-booking discounts.
- Whether the Japan Rail Pass special ticket for Nozomi and Mizuho will change.

Osaka is the main city of the Kansai region and the arrival point for many visitors flying into Kansai International Airport. This article covers Osaka Castle's main tower, the Dotonbori and Kuromon Market food areas as described by the Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau, the Osaka Amazing Pass, the train options from the airport, and the accommodation tax.

## Osaka Castle main tower

The Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau calls the main tower "the symbol of Osaka". It stands 55 metres above ground, with five tiers and eight floors. Inside is the Osaka Castle Museum, whose collection relates to Toyotomi Hideyoshi and the Sengoku period. Exhibits in the display rooms change.

The museum's own site gives these details, with hours and fees revised from 1 April 2025:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Opening hours | 9:00 to 18:00, last entry 17:30 |
| Closed | 28 December to 1 January |
| Adults | 1,200 yen |
| High school and university students | 600 yen, with student ID |
| Junior high school age and under | Free, with proof of age |

Students without ID pay the adult fee. The museum says overseas visitors aged 15 or under can show a passport as proof of age. Tickets can also be bought in advance through the museum's booking site.

**Getting there.** The tourism bureau gives a 15- to 20-minute walk from Morinomiya or Osakajokoen Station on the JR Osaka Loop Line.

## Dotonbori

The tourism bureau describes Dotonbori as "the popular shopping district of Minami". The name comes from Doton Yasui, who spent his fortune on digging the river in 1612. The area grew as an entertainment district of theatres, kabuki and teahouses, which led to its present reputation for food and live entertainment. The bureau notes the giant three-dimensional signs along the street, the Shochikuza theatre near Ebisubashi, and the Tombori Riverwalk beside the river.

**Getting there.** The bureau says Dotonbori is right outside Namba Station on the Nankai and Kintetsu railways. It also lists the Osaka Metro Midosuji, Sennichimae and Yotsubashi lines.

## Kuromon Market

Kuromon Market is a covered market running about 580 metres south from Sennichimae, with about 150 shops. The tourism bureau says it has both wholesale and retail businesses serving local people. It gives about 18,000 visitors on weekdays and about 150,000 at the end of the year.

The market dates from around 1822 to 1823, when merchants sold fish from Sakai and Kishu near Enmyoji temple. It was destroyed in the Great Namba Fire of 1912 and again in the wartime air raids, and was rebuilt after the war. Opening hours and closing days vary by shop.

**Getting there.** The bureau says it is right outside Nippombashi Station on the Osaka Metro Sennichimae Line.

## Getting around: the Osaka Amazing Pass

The Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau's old pass site states that its own sales of the Osaka Amazing Pass have closed, and links to the new version. The new pass is a digital ticket sold by SURUTTO KANSAI through its SURUTTO QRtto app.

| Version | Price |
|---|---|
| 1day | 3,500 yen |
| 2day | 5,000 yen |
| Osaka Itami Airport version, 1day / 2day | 3,800 yen / 5,400 yen |
| Kansai Airport version, 28 hours | 5,300 yen |

Prices are the same for adults and children. What the pass page lists:

- **Transport.** Unlimited rides on Osaka Metro, Osaka City Bus (some routes excluded), the New Tram, and Hankyu, Hanshin, Keihan, Kintetsu and Nankai trains mainly within Osaka City.
- **Attractions.** Entry to about 40 sightseeing spots.
- **Dates.** The 1day and 2day passes are on sale from 25 March 2026 to 31 March 2027. The 2day pass is for two consecutive days. A "day" runs from 3:00 to 2:59 the next morning, so a pass started between midnight and 2:59 cannot be used after 3:00.
- **Kansai Airport version.** On sale from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027. It is valid for 28 hours from first use and includes one one-way Nankai trip from Kansai Airport Station to Namba. It cannot be used from Namba to the airport. Reserved-seat limited express trains need an extra charge.

There are no refunds once you start using the pass. If you would rather pay as you go, see [IC cards: Suica, PASMO, ICOCA](/articles/ic-cards-suica-pasmo-icoca).

## From Kansai Airport

**Nankai.** Nankai gives Kansai Airport to Namba as 34 minutes at fastest on the Limited Express Rapi:t, and about 45 minutes on the Airport Express.

**JR West.** JR West runs two services from Kansai Airport Station:

- **Kansai-Airport Express HARUKA.** JR West describes it as giving direct access to Kyoto. Both a fare and a separate limited express fee are needed. Reserved seats are in dedicated HARUKA cars. JR West's WEST QR HARUKA one-way ticket for foreign visitors covers HARUKA plus JR conventional lines from Kansai Airport to Tennoji, Osaka, Shin-Osaka, Kobe, Nara or Kyoto. Its page notes that prices changed for tickets sold from 1 November 2026, so check the current fare before you buy.
- **Kansai-Airport Rapid Service.** JR West describes this as direct access to Osaka (Umeda and Tennoji stations). The train divides at Hineno, so when travelling to the airport, board one of the first four cars.

Rail passes can be bought or collected at the JR West ticket office at Kansai-airport Station. For journey times and pass coverage on HARUKA, see the [rail routes table](/rail-routes) and [rail and the JR Pass](/guide/rail-and-the-jr-pass).

## Accommodation tax

Osaka Prefecture charges an accommodation tax per person per night. For stays from 1 September 2025, the rates are:

| Room charge per person per night | Tax |
|---|---|
| Under 5,000 yen | Not taxed |
| 5,000 to under 15,000 yen | 200 yen |
| 15,000 to under 20,000 yen | 400 yen |
| 20,000 yen and over | 500 yen |

The tax is paid to the accommodation along with the room charge. The prefecture warns that a prepaid booking may not include it. If you prepaid at the old rates for a stay on or after 1 September 2025, you owe the difference. School trips and the official duties of foreign diplomatic missions are exempt.

## Planning notes

- Osaka Castle's main tower is closed from 28 December to 1 January.
- The Amazing Pass is a smartphone ticket. The pass site says to show its QR code at ticket gates, when getting off some buses and at attractions, and to allow extra time for this.
- Kyoto is a common next stop. See [getting around Kyoto](/articles/getting-around-kyoto), and for Nara, the [Nara day trip](/articles/nara-day-trip).

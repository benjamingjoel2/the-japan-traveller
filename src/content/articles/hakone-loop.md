Hakone is a hot-spring area in Kanagawa Prefecture. Visitors can travel round it on a loop of mountain railway, cable car, ropeway, lake boat and bus, all covered by one pass. This article explains the loop, the Hakone Freepass, the two ways in from Tokyo and the volcanic-gas rules at Owakudani.

## Getting there from Tokyo

There are two main routes, and they use different companies.

- **Odakyu from Shinjuku.** The Romancecar limited express runs directly from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto in about 80 minutes, according to Odakyu; ordinary express trains take about two hours. Every Romancecar seat is reserved. You pay a limited express surcharge (1,200 yen one way for adults to Hakone-Yumoto) on top of a normal fare, a pass or an IC card such as Suica or PASMO. Seats go on sale at 10:00 Japan time one month before departure, at stations or online through Odakyu's EMot ticket service.
- **JR to Odawara.** Take the JR Tokaido Shinkansen from Tokyo Station to Odawara, then the Hakone Tozan Railway to Hakone-Yumoto. JNTO gives about 55 minutes for this route.

Odakyu and the Hakone Tozan lines are private railways, not JR, so the Japan Rail Pass does not cover them. See the [rail guide](/guide/rail-and-the-jr-pass) and [Is the JR Pass still worth it?](/questions/is-the-jr-pass-still-worth-it).

## The loop, stage by stage

The legs below follow the order described by Hakone Navi, the Odakyu Group's visitor site, starting from Hakone-Yumoto.

1. **Hakone Tozan Train, Hakone-Yumoto to Gora.** A mountain railway known for its switchbacks, which it needs to climb the steep slopes.
2. **Hakone Tozan Cable Car, Gora to Sounzan.** A cable car through woodland. Hydrangeas flower along the line from mid-June to mid-July.
3. **Hakone Ropeway, Sounzan to Togendai via Owakudani and Ubako.** Each stage takes about 15 minutes, and passengers riding the whole line change gondolas at Owakudani. Gondolas carry 18 people and leave about every minute. Boarding hours are 9:00 to 17:00 from February to November and 9:00 to 16:15 in December and January. The ropeway can stop in winds of 30 m/s or more, in severe weather and for maintenance.
4. **Hakone Sightseeing Cruise, Togendai-ko across Lake Ashi.** The boats' interiors are modelled on British and French military ships, and JNTO notes that some are pirate-ship shaped. They run to Hakonemachi-ko, and Motohakone-ko also has a cruise ticket counter.
5. **Hakone Tozan Bus back.** From Motohakone-ko, buses run to Hakone-Yumoto and Odawara stations, either via Miyanoshita or via Hakone-machi-ko. From Togendai, buses also run to Hakone-Yumoto and Odawara via Sengoku and Miyanoshita.

Hakone Navi does not publish a total time for the loop, so check the timetables linked from its station pages when planning.

## The Hakone Freepass

Odakyu's Hakone Freepass gives unlimited rides on eight types of transport: the Odakyu Line, Hakone Tozan Train, Hakone Tozan Cable Car, Hakone Ropeway, Hakone Sightseeing Cruise, and Hakone Tozan Bus, Odakyu Highway Bus and Tokai Bus within designated areas. It also gives discounts at about 70 facilities, including hot springs and museums.

Prices on Odakyu's page as accessed on 7 November 2026 (the page gives no "as of" date):

| Pass | From Shinjuku (adult / child) | From Odawara (adult / child) |
|---|---|---|
| 2-day | 7,100 / 1,600 yen | 6,000 / 1,500 yen |
| 3-day | 7,500 / 1,850 yen | 6,400 / 1,750 yen |

- Child fares are for ages 6 to 11. Children under 6 do not need a ticket.
- The pass runs by calendar day, not by hours. A 2-day pass bought today is valid until the last service tomorrow.
- The Romancecar surcharge is not included. From Shinjuku it is 1,200 yen for adults one way to Hakone-Yumoto.
- You can buy the pass online through EMot as a smartphone ticket, or as a paper ticket from machines and Odakyu Sightseeing Service Centers.

## Owakudani and volcanic activity

Owakudani is a volcanic valley with steam vents, reached by the ropeway. JNTO says the area is sometimes closed because of volcanic activity and gas, and that the latest information should be checked on the official website. The JNTO National Parks site says ropeway operation is suspended when volcanic activity reaches an unsafe level.

The Hakone Ropeway's own notice says gas levels are constantly monitored, and that people with allergic asthma, bronchial, lung or heart conditions, heart pacemakers or poor physical health should not ride to Owakudani Station, as it may be life-threatening. It asks pregnant women, families with babies and small children, people with allergies and older people to consider carefully whether to ride.

Sections also close for scheduled maintenance. Hakone Ropeway's September 2026 notice lists:

- Owakudani to Sounzan: closed 7 to 24 December 2026, with replacement buses taking about 10 minutes.
- Togendai to Ubako to Owakudani: closed 12 January to 26 February 2027, with replacement buses taking about 15 minutes.

Replacement buses leave about every 15 minutes, and Hakone Freepass holders can use them.

- Check Hakone Navi's transport information page on the day for suspensions.
- If the ropeway is closed, check whether replacement buses run or use the Hakone Tozan Bus.

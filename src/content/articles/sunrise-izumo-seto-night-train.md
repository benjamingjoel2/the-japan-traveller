The Sunrise Izumo and Sunrise Seto are overnight sleeper limited express trains between Tokyo and western Japan. A March 2026 newspaper report describes them as the only sleeper train in Japan that runs on a regular schedule. This article covers the route, the 2026 times, the types of room, how to book, and what the Japan Rail Pass does and does not cover.

## Route

The two trains run as one 14-car train between Tokyo and Okayama, then divide. The Sunrise Seto continues to Takamatsu in Shikoku; the Sunrise Izumo continues to Izumoshi in Shimane. In the other direction they join at Okayama for the run to Tokyo.

JR West lists these stops, noting that stops vary by train:

- **Shared section:** Tokyo, Yokohama, Atami, Numazu, Fuji, Shizuoka, Hamamatsu, Osaka, Sannomiya, Himeji, Okayama.
- **Sunrise Seto:** Okayama, Kojima, Sakaide, Takamatsu.
- **Sunrise Izumo:** Okayama, Kurashiki, Bitchu-Takahashi, Niimi, Yonago, Yasugi, Matsue, Shinji, Izumoshi.

JR West's timetable notes that on certain dates the Sunrise Seto is extended to Kotohira.

## Times

A timetable change took effect on 14 March 2026. According to the Akita Sakigake Shimpo, the westbound departure from Tokyo moved earlier, from 21:50 to 21:26, to free time at night for track maintenance, and the journey became 24 minutes longer.

JR West's online timetable for October 2026 shows these times, with both trains listed as running daily:

| Westbound | Time |
|---|---|
| Tokyo, depart | 21:26 |
| Yokohama, depart | 21:52 |
| Okayama, arrive | 06:27 |
| Takamatsu, arrive (Sunrise Seto) | 07:27 |
| Izumoshi, arrive (Sunrise Izumo) | 10:00 |

| Eastbound | Time |
|---|---|
| Izumoshi, depart (Sunrise Izumo) | 18:57 |
| Takamatsu, depart (Sunrise Seto) | 21:26 |
| Tokyo, arrive | 07:08 |

The westbound train passes Osaka during the night without stopping. Eastbound, it calls at Osaka after midnight. JR West's timetable warns that services can be cancelled or retimed at short notice, so check the times for your date.

## Rooms and the Nobinobi seat

Almost all of the train is made up of private compartments. JR West's booking page lists six types of accommodation:

| Type | Class | Who can use it |
|---|---|---|
| Single Deluxe | A sleeper, private room | 1 person |
| Single | B sleeper, private room | 1 person |
| Solo | B sleeper, private room | 1 person |
| Single Twin | B sleeper, private room | Up to 2 (the second person uses an extra bed) |
| Sunrise Twin | B sleeper, two-person room | 1 or 2 people; charged per room |
| Nobinobi seat | Ordinary car | 1 to 6 people per booking |

JR West states that a Sunrise Twin booked for one person still needs two adult fare tickets.

The **Nobinobi seat** is not a bed in a room. JR West classes it as an ordinary-car seat, set on upper and lower levels in cars 5 and 12, with 14 places on each level of each car. For what a Japan Rail Pass covers, see below.

JR West's facilities list for the train includes a shower room, power outlets in the A and B private rooms only, multipurpose toilets, a wheelchair-accessible room and seat, women-only seats, vending machines and Wi-Fi.

## Booking

JR West's English reservation conditions state that online reservations open at 10:00 a.m. (Japan time) one month before the day of travel. They also confirm that the private rooms on the Sunrise Seto and Sunrise Izumo can be booked online through JR West's e5489 service, as an exception to the rule that special facilities cannot be reserved online. All six accommodation types appear on JR West's e5489 booking page for the train.

Two practical points from JR West's booking page:

- If you board on the Osaka side after midnight, search using the date the train left its starting station, with a departure time in the 23:00 hour.
- Ticket offices at some intermediate stations close before the train calls, so collect tickets in advance.

## The Japan Rail Pass and the Sunrise

The Japan Rail Pass official site's conditions of use state that to travel in a sleeping car, pass holders must pay the sleeping car charge as well as the limited express charge. If a private compartment is Green Car accommodation, the Green Car charge is also payable. The pass does not allow you to change to a reserved seat or a sleeping car after boarding.

The official pass pages do not mention the Sunrise by name, so they do not say what a pass holder pays for a room or a Nobinobi seat on this train. Ask at a JR Ticket Office when you reserve. The pass site states that pass holders can reserve seats at a ticket office (Midori-no-madoguchi), at a reserved seat ticket machine, or online if the pass was bought on the official site.

For the wider picture of how the pass works and what it costs, see [Rail in Japan and the Japan Rail Pass](/guide/rail-and-the-jr-pass) and the [rail-route table](/rail-routes).

## Planning notes

- Bookings open at 10:00 a.m. Japan time, one month before travel.
- Times changed in March 2026; older guides show a 21:50 departure from Tokyo.
- Westbound, the train does not stop at Osaka. Eastbound, the Osaka call is after midnight.
- The Japan Rail Pass does not cover sleeping car charges on the private rooms.
- Check your date on JR West's timetable; services can be cancelled at short notice.

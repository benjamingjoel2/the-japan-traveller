The reconstructed Seiden, the main hall of Shurijo Castle in Naha, opens to the public on 23 November 2026. Shurijo Castle Park announced the opening date on 4 June 2026, and on 31 July 2026 it announced that entry to the hall would need a timed advance booking and that admission fees would rise from the opening day. The park's site says the hall is due to open at noon on 23 November.

## What has changed

The Okinawa Convention and Visitors Bureau (OCVB) describes the hall as rising again after the fire of 2019. From 23 November, the park splits its paid area into two kinds of ticket:

- **Admission ticket with Seiden interior reservation.** Needed if you want to go inside the main hall. You choose a date and time slot when you book. There is no extra charge for the time slot.
- **Admission ticket without reservation.** Covers the paid area except the inside of the Seiden. The park says you can still see the hall's exterior from a viewing deck and visit the other exhibits. It can be bought online or at the ticket office on the day.

The fee for the paid area is the same whichever ticket you choose. Visitors who are admitted free, such as Okinawa residents aged 70 or over and holders of disability certificates, still need a booking to enter the Seiden. Preschool children do not.

## New admission fees

The park says the increase reflects stronger fire and disaster prevention and higher running costs. Individual fees (fewer than 20 people), including tax:

| Visitor | Until 21 November 2026 | From 23 November 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Adult | ¥400 | ¥1,000 |
| High school student | ¥300 | ¥760 |
| Elementary and junior high school student | ¥160 | ¥370 |
| Under 6 | Free | Free |

Group rates (20 or more) from 23 November are ¥800, ¥610 and ¥300. The park also says that showing a Yui Rail one-day or two-day pass at the ticket window gets the group rate.

## Booking and timing

According to the park and OCVB:

- Tickets with a Seiden reservation for 23 November to 31 December 2026 went on sale in two rounds, from 13:00 on 1 September and from 13:00 on 1 October. Sales are first come, first served.
- For visits from 1 January 2027, sales open at 13:00 on the 1st of the month two months before the visit date.
- If slots remain, same-day tickets can be bought online up to 15 minutes before the slot, or at the Kofukumon ticket office.
- The park will not take group bookings for the Seiden interior from 23 November to 6 December 2026. Groups are admitted in stages from 7 December.
- Shoes are removed inside the hall, and the park asks visitors to bring a shoe bag.

The park suggests booking a slot about 40 to 50 minutes after you expect to arrive at Shuri Station on the monorail. It estimates a full visit, including the hall, at about 90 to 120 minutes, of which about 15 minutes is inside the Seiden. It recommends public transport because it expects congestion on nearby roads, and says it may limit entry on busy days. For the monorail itself, see our [Okinawa article](/articles/okinawa-when-to-go).

## Closures around the opening

Shurijo Castle Park closes all day on 22 November 2026, when a completion ceremony for the reconstruction is held, and on the morning of 23 November.

## What we don't know yet

On 9 October 2026 the park posted a notice that its foreign-language pages were temporarily unavailable, so booking details are currently given in Japanese. We could not confirm how many visitors each time slot holds, or how long the reservation system will stay in place. OCVB's English notice does not list fees or hours.

On 13 October 2026 seven railway operators in eastern Japan announced that they will replace magnetic paper tickets with tickets carrying a QR code from spring 2027. The seven are JR East, Keisei Electric Railway, Keikyu, Tobu Railway, Tokyo Monorail, Hokuso Railway and Sendai Airport Transit. The change affects people who buy single tickets from station machines. It does not affect IC cards such as Suica and PASMO. The operators give the aim of keeping rail services sustainable in the long term as their reason for moving away from magnetic tickets.

## What changes for single tickets

Today a paper ticket is fed into the slot of the ticket gate. Under the new system:

- you still buy the ticket from a station ticket machine;
- at the entry gate you hold the QR code over the reader instead of inserting the ticket;
- at the exit gate you hold it over the reader again, then drop the ticket into a collection box on the gate.

The service will cover all lines and all stations of the seven operators. At stations without gates that can read QR tickets, you go to a staffed window instead. The operators say each company will give details later.

Most QR tickets from ticket machines will be the large format, 57.5 mm by 85 mm. Today's magnetic tickets are the small format, 30 mm by 57.5 mm. The operators say there are some exceptions.

The release says the operators will keep building their ticketless services around transport IC cards, Mobile Suica and Mobile PASMO. Nothing in the announcement changes how Suica or PASMO work. See [IC cards: Suica, PASMO and ICOCA](/articles/ic-cards-suica-pasmo-icoca).

## IC cards and other railways

The plan began in May 2024 as a joint announcement by eight operators. The October 2026 release names seven for the spring 2027 start. It says any operator starting in fiscal 2027 or later will announce this itself once decided. It also says the seven are working with the other railways they run through services with. Railways not named in the release, such as Tokyo Metro, are not part of this change.

## What we don't know yet

- The exact start date in spring 2027 for each operator and station.
- When each operator stops selling magnetic tickets, and what happens to other paper products. The operators say they will announce changes to magnetic ticket services later.
- How QR tickets will work on through services onto railways outside the group.

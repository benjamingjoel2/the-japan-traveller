Takachiho is a mountain town in the north of Miyazaki Prefecture, known for its river gorge and for shrines tied to the myth of the sun goddess Amaterasu. This article covers the gorge and its rental boats, the nightly kagura dance performance at Takachiho Shrine, Amano Iwato Shrine, and how to reach the town by bus. It can be combined with Aso; see [Kumamoto Castle and Mount Aso](/articles/kumamoto-castle-and-aso).

## Takachiho Gorge

JNTO says the gorge was formed over 100,000 years ago as a result of nearby volcanic activity. Its best-known feature is Manai Falls, which JNTO gives as 17 m high. A paved path runs along the edge of the gorge for 1 km. JNTO notes that the riverside walk is rocky and slippery in places, so wear sturdy shoes. JNTO also says the gorge is lit until 22:00 during the warmer months.

## Renting a boat

The only way to get close beneath the falls is by rowing boat. The details below are from the Takachiho Tourist Association's pages as of 7 November 2026.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Reception | 8:30 to 17:00, last check-in 16:30; may close earlier if a queue forms |
| Rental time | 30 minutes; each extra 10 minutes costs ¥1,000 |
| Fee | The association's fee calendar shows ¥5,100 for every day of November 2026 |
| Capacity | 3 people per boat, or 4 if the group includes a preschool child |
| Rowing | Only junior high school students and older may row |
| Not allowed | Pets, large luggage, standing up, changing rowers, drinking or smoking on board |

**Booking.** Reservations are made online only, through the association's booking site. Booking opens at 9:00 two weeks before the boarding date and closes at 9:00 two days before. Telephone bookings are not accepted. On days when online bookings sell out, no same-day tickets are sold and there is no waiting list. The association posts each day's status at around 8:00.

**Closures.** Boats do not run when the river is high after rain, during regular safety inspections in January, April, July and October (which may close the whole day), during emergency inspections after rain or typhoons, or after an earthquake of seismic intensity 3 or higher in Takachiho. Service can also stop part-way through a day if the river rises. Staff ask rowers to keep out of the area where the falls drop. If a life jacket inflates near Manai Falls, replacing its cylinder costs ¥3,000.

The association notes that the No. 1 Mishiroi car park is not a dedicated car park for boat users.

## Takachiho Shrine and the night kagura

JNTO says Takachiho Shrine was founded about 1,900 years ago and enshrines three generations of deities, including Ninigi no Mikoto. In the grounds are two tall cedars joined at the roots. JNTO says the shrine is just west of the town centre, about 15 minutes on foot from the bus centre.

**Takachiho Kagura.** Every night, a one-hour kagura performance is held in the shrine's kagura hall. According to the tourist association:

- Time: 20:00 to 21:00.
- Programme: four of the 33 Takachiho yokagura dances (Tajikarao no Mai, Uzume no Mai, Totori no Mai and Goshintai no Mai), which tell the story of Amaterasu and the Heavenly Rock Cave.
- Fee: ¥1,000 for adults; elementary school children and younger free. Groups of 20 or more pay ¥900 each, but not when booking online.
- Not held on 31 December and 1 January. On 22 and 23 November the regular performance is replaced by festival performances.
- Seating: about 300 places, first come, first served, on a tatami floor. There are no chairs, but you may bring a cushion or your own chair.
- Booking: about 230 places can be booked online from around 10:00, 30 days ahead, until 17:00 on the day, by credit card only, up to 10 people per booking. About 50 to 60 tickets are sold at the kagura hall from 19:30. Online ticket holders check in from 19:00.
- Cancellation: 10% from booking until two days before, 50% the day before and 100% on the day.
- Pets are not admitted.

**Yokagura season.** The full yokagura, which the association says is a national Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property, is performed in local kagura lodgings from mid-November to early February. JNTO says all 33 dances are performed from dusk to dawn.

## Amano Iwato Shrine

JNTO says Amano Iwato Shrine is dedicated to Amaterasu. Legend holds that she hid in a nearby cave, withholding her light from the world, until a dance made the other gods laugh and drew her out. Some of the shrine buildings face the cave across the Iwato River. From a place of worship behind the shrine, visitors can see the cave with the guidance of a priest.

Following the river path, lined with small stone piles, leads to Amano Yasukawara, the cave where the gods are said to have gathered. JNTO puts it at a 10-minute walk from the shrine. The shrine is about 10 to 15 minutes from central Takachiho by car, bus or taxi, and JNTO says a bus leaves the Takachiho Bus Center about once an hour. Kagura is also performed at this shrine at its spring festival in early May, its autumn festival in late September and on Culture Day, 3 November.

## Getting to Takachiho

There is no railway to Takachiho. JNTO says the town is not easy to reach by public transport. The tourist association lists these bus routes:

| From | Route | Journey time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kumamoto | Express bus Aso-go / Takachiho-go | About 3 hours | 1 trip a day; advance booking required |
| Aso Station | Asochiho bus | About 1 hour 50 minutes | Advance booking required |
| Kumamoto Airport | Yamabiko express bus to Aso Station (about 50 minutes) or Kyushu Odan bus (about 1 hour), then Asochiho | | Change at Aso Station |
| Nobeoka Station | Local bus | About 1 hour 30 minutes | |
| Fukuoka Airport | Gokase-go | About 3 hours 30 minutes | 4 trips a day; advance booking required |

From 1 July 2026, Aso City's tourism site says the Asochiho bus runs from Aso Station via the Kusasenri Volcano Museum, Aso Sanjo Terminal, Shirakawa Spring, Takamori Yusui Tunnel Park and Takamori Station to the Takachiho Bus Center, and that reserved passengers have priority. By car, JNTO gives Miyazaki City to Takachiho as 140 km, about 2.5 hours using the toll road.

## Before you go

- Boats sell out at busy times. Book exactly two weeks ahead at 9:00, and have a plan for the gorge path if the river is too high.
- Night kagura runs from 20:00 to 21:00. Check how you will get back to your accommodation afterwards; JNTO notes that many hotels run free shuttles to the performance.
- Check bus timetables and book seats before travelling. Several routes run only once a day.

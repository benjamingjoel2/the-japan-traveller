The Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route crosses the Northern Alps between Toyama Prefecture and Nagano Prefecture. It is a chain of cable cars, buses, a ropeway and a short walk. This page sets out the vehicles in order, the 2026 season and Snow Wall Walk dates, how web tickets and boarding-time reservations work, and the official baggage forwarding service.

## What the route is

The route runs between Tateyama Station on the Toyama side and Ogizawa on the Nagano side, with a local bus on to Shinano-Omachi Station. Private vehicles are not allowed between Tateyama Station and Ogizawa. The operator encourages drivers to use a vehicle transfer service.

The official transportation page gives these segments, from west to east:

| # | Segment | Vehicle | Time | Distance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tateyama Station to Bijodaira | Tateyama Cable Car | 7 min | 1.3 km |
| 2 | Bijodaira to Murodo | Tateyama Highland Bus | 50 min | 23 km |
| 3 | Murodo to Daikanbo | Tateyama Tunnel Electric Bus | 10 min | 3.7 km |
| 4 | Daikanbo to Kurobedaira | Tateyama Ropeway | 7 min | 1.7 km |
| 5 | Kurobedaira to Kurobeko | Kurobe Cable Car | 5 min | 0.8 km |
| 6 | Kurobeko to Kurobe Dam | On foot | 15 min | |
| 7 | Kurobe Dam to Ogizawa | Kanden Tunnel Electric Bus | 16 min | 6.1 km |
| 8 | Ogizawa to Shinano-Omachi | Local bus | 40 min | 18 km |

The operator notes some features of individual segments:

- The Tateyama Cable Car climbs 502 m at an average gradient of 24 degrees.
- The Highland Bus can be left at Midagahara or Tengudaira. A reservation is needed at each station to board again.
- The Tateyama Ropeway has no pylons along its span.
- The Kurobe Cable Car runs underground.

The times above do not include waiting or transfers. The operator's access page states that its train times "do not include transfer times".

JNTO describes the Kurobe Dam as 186 metres tall and Japan's tallest dam, completed in 1963. Walking across it is part of the route. In 2026 the operator listed scenic water releases at the dam from 26 June to 15 October. Times varied by period, and releases can be suspended without notice.

## The 2026 season

The operator announced in January 2026 that the route would open on 15 April 2026 and operate until 30 November 2026. The 2026 timetables split the season in two:

- **15 April to 3 November 2026:** the main timetable, in both directions between Toyama and Nagano.
- **4 to 30 November 2026:** a winter schedule. The timetable page lists this period for travel from Tateyama Station to Ogizawa and back. Web tickets for 11 to 30 November are limited to round trips from Ogizawa, to Murodo, Daikanbo, Kurobedaira or Kurobe Dam, plus a one-way option from Ogizawa to Kurobe Dam.

As of 7 November 2026 the operator had not announced dates for the 2027 season. On 7 November 2026 the operator warned that the Highland Bus might be suspended the next day because of snow.

## Yuki-no-Otani and the Snow Wall Walk

The snow corridor at Murodo is open to walkers in spring. The operator calls the event the Snow Wall Walk. Its 2026 leaflet gives:

- **Dates:** 15 April to 25 June 2026
- **Hours:** 09:30 to 15:00
- **Place:** Murodo. The entrance to the snow wall area is on the first floor of Murodo Terminal.
- **Admission:** free, but Alpine Route tickets are needed to reach Murodo.

The operator advises:

- Bring winter clothes. It may snow, and the highest temperature may be below 10 degrees C.
- Bring sunglasses and sunscreen.
- Wear trekking shoes or snow boots.
- Do not fly drones anywhere on the route.

It also warns that peak-hour services can fill up. Plan arrival times with the walk's closing time in mind.

## Tickets and reservations

The operator recommends buying web tickets in advance. A WEB Ticket combines the fare with a reserved boarding time. Only two departures have fixed boarding times:

- the Tateyama Cable Car from Tateyama Station
- the Kanden Tunnel Electric Bus from Ogizawa

At other stations, passengers go straight to the ticket gate and take whatever service is running.

Points from the WEB Ticket site, as of November 2026:

- Standard web tickets go on sale at 15:00 in staggered windows, usually covering about ten days of travel. The sales calendar is published on the operator's site.
- The ticket is issued at the departure station from 12:00 on the day before travel.
- Payment is by credit card. The site asks buyers not to use debit or prepaid cards because refunds can take a long time.
- Cancelling a standard ticket after 15:00 on the day before costs 500 yen per person. After the departure time the full fare is charged.
- Option tickets for the Ogizawa to Nagano express bus and the Ogizawa to Shinano-Omachi local bus can be added to a web ticket. They cannot be bought on their own.

If web tickets are sold out, same-day tickets are sold at Dentetsu Toyama, Tateyama Station and Ogizawa, for cash or card. They are valid only on the day of purchase and cannot be bought in advance. For overnight stays on the route the ticket is valid for five days from purchase, but it does not allow unlimited rides. The operator warns that sales may be restricted in early May, mid-August and early October.

## Luggage

The operator runs a same-day baggage forwarding service. In 2026 it ran from 15 April to 8 November. Fees are per item, tax included, and payable in cash only:

| Between | Fee |
|---|---|
| Dentetsu Toyama Station or Tateyama Station-area hotels and Shinano-Omachi Station or Omachi Onsen | 4,000 yen |
| Dentetsu Toyama Station and Tateyama Station-area hotels | 2,000 yen |
| Shinano-Omachi Station and Omachi Onsen | 2,000 yen |

No reservation is needed. The operator asks customers to reach the counter 20 to 30 minutes before departure.

| Station | Drop-off | Pick-up |
|---|---|---|
| Dentetsu Toyama | 05:00 to 10:00 | 15:30 to 18:00 |
| Shinano-Omachi | 07:40 to 10:30 | 15:00 to 17:45 |

Bags can be refused for excessive weight or size. No numeric limit is published.

On the Highland Bus, large backpacks go in the luggage compartment. The operator asks passengers to protect valuables and to cover skis, snowboards and similar items. Bear spray should be sealed in a plastic bag inside a pack, with the safety pin secured.

## Planning notes

- Check the operator's announcements page shortly before travel for weather suspensions and timetable changes.
- On the Nagano side, construction on the Omachi Alpine Line was causing delays in 2026. The Ogizawa to Nagano express bus had limited departures, so check its first and last times.

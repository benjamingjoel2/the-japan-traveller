Miraikan, the National Museum of Emerging Science and Innovation in Tokyo, closed on 1 October 2026 for a refurbishment of the whole building. On 16 September 2026 the museum confirmed that its grand reopening will be on Friday 23 April 2027. Anyone planning a visit to Miraikan this winter or early spring should note that the museum will be shut throughout.

## What is closed

The museum first announced the closure on 27 March 2025. That notice gave the closure as about six months, from 1 October 2026 to mid-April 2027, and said the whole museum is closed. The reason given is the age of the building and facilities. Miraikan opened in July 2001, and in its September 2026 notice it says its total visitor number reached 20 million in May 2025.

The September 2026 notice says the aim is more than a simple update of the facilities, with new exhibitions and services planned for the reopening. There is nothing inside to visit during the closure. The museum's 2025 notice says it will keep up science communication work through off-site events and information online, and points to its website and official social media for details.

## What changes on reopening

The museum's 16 September 2026 overview lists what it plans to open in April 2027:

- New permanent exhibitions on themes including genomes and the brain
- Special exhibitions planned by Miraikan itself
- New programmes at the Dome Theater, which shows full-dome 3D films and planetarium shows
- An update to the museum's partner robot, "Keparan"
- A renewed Curiosity Field, the children's area for children and parents
- A restaurant and cafe on a new concept, and a larger museum shop
- New, more consistent signs throughout the building

The museum's home page also lists a special exhibition titled "New Bodies. New Selves." for spring 2027.

## Planning notes

- The museum's address is 2-3-6 Aomi, Koto-ku, Tokyo.
- If your trip falls before 23 April 2027, plan another indoor stop in Tokyo instead.
- Opening hours, admission fees and the dates of the spring 2027 special exhibition after reopening had not been published in English when we checked. The museum says further details will come in press releases and on its website.

## What we don't know yet

The museum has not yet published new admission prices, opening hours or any booking rules for the reopened building. We could not confirm whether any of the existing permanent exhibitions will be withdrawn, beyond the museum's statement that some will be renewed.

The Museum of the Imperial Collections, Sannomaru Shozokan, fully opened its new building on 3 November 2026. The museum sits inside the East Gardens of the Imperial Palace in central Tokyo. Its three opening exhibitions, running into May 2027, use a date-and-time ticket system, and the museum says tickets cannot be bought at the venue on the day.

## What has reopened

The museum first opened in November 1993 to hold works of art formerly owned by the Imperial Family. According to its own history page, construction of a new building with more storage and exhibition space began in 2019. Management passed from the Imperial Household Agency to the National Institutes for Cultural Heritage in 2023, and the new building partly opened in November of that year before opening fully in November 2026.

The museum lists three "Grand Opening" special exhibitions:

| Exhibition | Dates | General admission |
|---|---|---|
| Beauty Through the Ages: Treasures of the Imperial Collections | 3 November 2026 to 24 January 2027 | ¥2,500 |
| Sacred Garden of Birds and Flowers: Ito Jakuchu's Colorful Realm of Living Beings and Painting in Kyoto | 4 February to 14 March 2027 | ¥3,000 |
| Japanese Calligraphy: A 1,300-Year Legacy of Beauty | 25 March to 16 May 2027 | ¥2,500 |

Prices include tax. The first exhibition changes its works in three rotations: 3 to 23 November, 26 November to 20 December, and 23 December 2026 to 24 January 2027. The museum says it includes National Treasures, Important Cultural Properties and items from the Shosoin and Horyuji treasures.

## Booking rules

- Entry is only on the date and time slot you reserve. Remaining availability is shown on the online booking site, and the museum asks visitors not to phone about it.
- Admission is free for visitors under 23 and for university, high school and junior high school students, but they must still book in advance and show ID or a student card.
- Elementary school children and younger, and visitors with disabilities plus one caregiver, are free without a booking.
- The museum warns there may still be a wait to enter at busy times.

## Hours and access

The museum is open 9:30 to 17:00 (last admission 16:30), and until 20:00 on Fridays and Saturdays (last admission 19:30). It closes on Mondays (or the next weekday if Monday is a holiday), over the New Year holidays, on the Emperor's Birthday and between exhibitions. For the first exhibition the listed closures are Mondays, 24 and 25 November, 22 December, 28 December to 3 January, and 12 January 2027, with the museum open on 23 November 2026 and 11 January 2027.

The nearest entrance is Ote-mon Gate, about 100 metres from the museum. The museum gives these walking times:

- About 5 minutes from Exit C13a of Otemachi Station (subway)
- About 15 minutes from the Marunouchi North Exit of Tokyo Station (JR)

Bags are security-screened at the gates. On Fridays the rest of the East Gardens is closed, and museum visitors must enter through Ote-mon Gate. The Hirakawa-mon and Kita-Hanebashi-mon gates are also closed on Mondays and Fridays.

## Restricted access, 28 November to 6 December

In a notice dated 6 November 2026, the museum says daytime access to the East Gardens will be restricted from Saturday 28 November to Sunday 6 December, because of the autumn public opening of Inui-dori Street in the palace grounds. Check the museum's notice before booking a slot in that period.

## What we don't know yet

The museum has not published, in English, how many tickets each time slot holds, or whether timed tickets will continue after the opening exhibitions end in May 2027.

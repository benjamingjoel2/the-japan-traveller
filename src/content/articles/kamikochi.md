Kamikochi is a high valley on the Azusa River in Nagano Prefecture, inside Chubu-Sangaku National Park. It is open to visitors for about seven months a year, and private cars are never allowed in. This page covers the 2026 season dates, how to get in by shuttle bus or taxi, the main flat walks from Kappa-bashi, and the park's rules, including official bear guidance.

## The open season

For 2026, the official Kamikochi site gives the season as opening on 17 April, with a grand opening ceremony on 27 April, and closing on 15 November. The FAQ describes the season as "mid-April to mid-November": bus services from Matsumoto start on 17 April, not every facility opens on that day, and buses and facilities close on 16 November after running through 15 November.

Facilities close in stages before the end. The Kamikochi Tourism Association's closing notice for 2026 lists the closing ceremony on 15 November and the last nights for accommodation as follows:

| Last night of stay (2026) | Facilities |
|---|---|
| 31 October | Myojinkan |
| 3 November | Morino Resort Konashi, Yamanohidaya, Tokusawaen, Yokoo Sanso |
| 4 November | Kamonjigoya, Tokusawa Lodge |
| 13 November | Taishoike Hotel |
| 14 November | Most hotels near Kappa-bashi and the bus terminal |

In winter, shops, hotels and other facilities are closed, and the Mount Yakedake trail is closed. The official site advises consulting a guide before a winter visit. Its rules page has separate winter guidance: submit a climbing registration form at Nakanoyu Gate, and watch for avalanches, falling rocks and snowdrifts, particularly on the road between Kamikochi Tunnel and Taisho-ike.

The FAQ lists spring (May to June), summer (July to August) and autumn (October) as the best seasons. See /guide/seasons for how these months fit the rest of the country.

## The private-car ban

Private cars, including rental cars and motorcycles, cannot enter Kamikochi at any time of year. The restriction begins beyond the Kama Tunnel. During the open season only buses, taxis and light vehicles may go through. Drivers park at one of two places and switch to a shuttle bus or taxi:

- **Sawando**, on the Matsumoto (Nagano) side. About 2,000 spaces across several lots. The Kamikochi Tourism Association gives the daily fee for a car as 800 yen. The English-language site gives 700 yen, so check on arrival.
- **Hirayu (Akandana parking)**, on the Takayama (Gifu) side. About 850 spaces, 600 yen per 24 hours for a car. Open 24 hours from 17 April to 15 November and closed in winter.

The association warns that summer weekends, holidays, Obon and the October foliage period are very crowded. It advises allowing extra time.

## Shuttle buses and taxis

The shuttle buses stop at Taisho-ike, in front of the Imperial Hotel, and at the Kamikochi Bus Terminal. The English site gives about 20 to 30 minutes from either parking area by bus or taxi. The association's pages list these fares but do not give the year they apply to:

| Shuttle bus | Adult one way | Adult round trip | Child round trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sawando to Kamikochi | 1,600 yen | 3,000 yen | 1,500 yen |
| Hirayu (Akandana) to Kamikochi | 1,500 yen | 2,800 yen | 1,400 yen |

Fixed taxi fares from the Sawando upper zone lots to the Kamikochi Bus Terminal are 5,400 yen in a standard vehicle (up to six people including the driver). From the Sawando lower zone the fare is 6,000 yen. From the Hirayu area the maximum fare is 6,000 yen, and the Azumi Tunnel toll is charged on top. The last taxi from Sawando leaves at 18:00. The association warns that taxis may not be waiting at Akandana after 17:00.

Without a car, the official site gives two main routes:

- **From Matsumoto:** Matsumoto Dentetsu train to Shin-Shimashima (30 minutes), then bus to Kamikochi (about 60 minutes). The site states that the bus back from Kamikochi to Shin-Shimashima or Matsumoto must be reserved, as must the direct bus from Matsumoto.
- **From Takayama:** Nohi bus from Takayama Bus Center to Hirayu Onsen (60 minutes), then bus or taxi to Kamikochi (25 minutes). See /articles/takayama for the town itself.

The JR Pass does not cover these buses or the Matsumoto-side train, according to the FAQ (see /guide/rail-and-the-jr-pass). The gate closes at 19:00 in spring and autumn and 20:00 in summer. The last bus leaves the bus terminal around 18:00.

## Kappa-bashi and the valley walks

Kappa-bashi is a suspension bridge over the Azusa River near the bus terminal. The association's history page says it was first built in 1910 and has been rebuilt several times. The Ministry of the Environment's bear card gives its length as about 37 metres. Taisho-ike, at the southern end of the valley, formed in 1915 when a debris flow from Mount Yakedake dammed the river.

The official walking page gives these times and distances from Kappa-bashi. Each route can be walked on either bank of the river:

| Route | Right bank | Left bank | Round trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kappa-bashi to Taisho-ike | 75 min, 3.8 km | 80 min, 4 km | about 2.5 hours |
| Kappa-bashi to Myojin | 60 min, 3.0 km | 50 min, 2.5 km | about 2 hours, 5.5 km |
| Myojin to Tokusawa | 60 min, 3.0 km | | Kappa-bashi to Tokusawa and back: 4 to 5 hours |

Shorter loops are also listed. The bus terminal to Kappa-bashi to Tashiro-bashi and back takes 55 minutes. A one-way walk from the Taisho-ike bus stop past Tashiro-ike and Tashiro-bashi to Kappa-bashi and the bus terminal takes 70 minutes.

## Rules and bear safety

Kamikochi was designated part of Chubu-Sangaku National Park on 4 December 1934. It has been a Special Scenic Place and Special Natural Monument since 29 March 1952. The official rules include:

- Do not feed wild animals, including monkeys and ducks.
- Take all rubbish home. The FAQ says rubbish bins are almost non-existent.
- Stay on the trail and keep out of marshes.
- Camping and fires are allowed only in designated areas.
- Do not collect plants or wildlife, including insects. This is illegal.
- No drones in the national park, no bicycles on the trails, and no pets.

Asiatic black bears live in the valley. The Ministry of the Environment's bilingual card for Kamikochi advises:

- Keep a moderate distance from bears.
- Carry bear bells and ring them so bears notice you.
- Do not approach or photograph a bear out of curiosity. Mother bears are especially dangerous.
- Do not leave food out and take all rubbish with you, because a bear that has tasted human food may become extremely dangerous.

Report sightings to the Kamikochi Information Center, +81-263-95-2433, open 08:00 to 17:00.

## Before you go

- There are no ATMs, banks or convenience stores in Kamikochi. Most places take major credit cards, but the FAQ advises carrying some cash.
- The bus terminal baggage room is open 06:00 to 17:00. There are no coin lockers.
- Check the official site for road closures. In 2026 the park road closed briefly in August after a fallen tree.

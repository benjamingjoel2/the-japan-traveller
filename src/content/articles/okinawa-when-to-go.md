Okinawa has a subtropical climate that differs from the rest of Japan: it is warm in winter, its rainy season comes earlier, and it sees more typhoons. This article sets out the weather month by month, when the cherry blossom and the rainy and typhoon seasons fall, and how to reach and move around the main island and the outlying islands. For how Okinawa fits with the rest of the country, see [the regions guide](/guide/regions) and the [month-by-month pages](/seasons).

## Climate through the year

The Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) publishes 30-year normals (1991 to 2020) for Naha, the capital. The figures below are monthly means.

| Month | Mean temperature | Precipitation |
|---|---|---|
| January | 17.3°C | 101.6 mm |
| February | 17.5°C | 114.5 mm |
| March | 19.1°C | 142.8 mm |
| April | 21.5°C | 161.0 mm |
| May | 24.2°C | 245.3 mm |
| June | 27.2°C | 284.4 mm |
| July | 29.1°C | 188.1 mm |
| August | 29.0°C | 240.0 mm |
| September | 27.9°C | 275.2 mm |
| October | 25.5°C | 179.2 mm |
| November | 22.5°C | 119.1 mm |
| December | 19.0°C | 110.0 mm |

The annual mean is 23.3°C and annual precipitation is 2,161.0 mm.

The Okinawa Convention and Visitors Bureau (OCVB) describes the climate as humid and subtropical. It says sea temperature ranges from around 21°C in February to nearly 30°C in August, and that many resort beaches open for swimming in March. Sudden showers are common, but it rarely rains all day. JNTO notes that Okinawa's summer stretches from June to October and advises UV protection from March to October. In winter, OCVB says coats and sweaters are occasionally needed, as northerly winds can make it feel colder.

## Rainy season and typhoons

JMA's normal dates for Okinawa's rainy season are a start around 10 May and an end around 21 June (the 1991 to 2020 average). Actual dates vary: in 2026 JMA put the start around 4 May and the end around 29 June. This is about a month earlier than on the main islands; see [Is Japan good in the rainy season?](/questions/is-japan-good-in-the-rainy-season)

Typhoons are the main weather risk for planning. JMA counts a typhoon as approaching Okinawa or Amami when its centre comes within 300 km of a weather station there. On the 1991 to 2020 averages, approaches to Okinawa and Amami are:

- July: 1.5 a year
- August: 2.3 a year (the highest month)
- September: 2.0 a year
- October: 1.1 a year
- June: 0.6; May: 0.4; November: 0.3

That gives 7.9 approaches a year on average. OCVB says there is a chance of typhoons from June through September. Ferries and flights can be affected by the weather; OCVB notes this for inter-island ferries. For more on typhoons across Japan, see [When is typhoon season in Japan?](/questions/when-is-typhoon-season-in-japan)

## Cherry blossom in winter

OCVB describes Okinawa's cherry blossom as the earliest in Japan, and JNTO says it blooms in late January and February. OCVB suggests the grounds of the Nakijin Castle Ruins during the blossom season. Winter is also humpback whale season: OCVB says the whales migrate to the Kerama Islands area to breed.

## Getting there

There is no JR line in Okinawa, so most visitors fly. JNTO says most flights to Naha Airport depart from Tokyo or Osaka, and that there are direct international flights from China, South Korea, Singapore, Taiwan and Hong Kong. Naha Airport is the gateway to all of the Okinawa islands. JNTO says central Naha is a 15-minute drive from the airport or a few stops on the monorail.

## Getting around the main island

**Yui Rail.** The Okinawa Urban Monorail (Yui Rail) has 19 stations, from Naha Airport to Tedako-Uranishi, and passes through central Naha and Shuri. The line opened in August 2003 and was extended into Urasoe in 2019. OCVB says it connects the 19 stations in around 40 minutes. Yui Rail sells two passes at all stations:

| Pass | Adult | Child | Valid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-Day Free Pass | ¥1,000 | ¥500 | 24 hours from purchase |
| 2-Day Free Pass | ¥1,800 | ¥900 | 48 hours from purchase |

The clock starts when you buy the pass, not at first use. Suica and the other nationwide IC cards (including PASMO, ICOCA, SUGOCA and nimoca) work on Yui Rail, as does the local OKICA card. Yui Rail does not sell or refund Suica cards.

**Buses.** JNTO describes a comprehensive bus service on the main island, with express buses to places outside Naha. OCVB lists local buses, airport limousine buses and shuttle buses.

**Car rental.** JNTO says the best way to get around Okinawa is by rental car, and that cars can be rented at the airport and in central Naha. OCVB recommends booking in advance.

## The outlying islands

**Kerama Islands.** JNTO says there are more than 20 islands in the group, of which four are inhabited. With the surrounding uninhabited islands they form Keramashoto National Park (OCVB). High-speed ferries leave from Tomari Port in Naha. JNTO lists about 40 minutes to Tokashiki (2 to 3 trips a day), 50 to 70 minutes to Zamami (2 trips) and about 50 minutes to Aka (1 to 2 trips). OCVB says the islands are close enough to visit as a day trip.

**Ishigaki.** JNTO describes Ishigaki as about 2,000 km from Tokyo. Direct flights from Haneda take about 3 hours, and there are frequent flights from Naha to New Ishigaki Airport. A bus links the airport and central Ishigaki in about 45 minutes.

**Miyako.** JNTO says the only way to reach Miyako Island is by plane, with direct flights from Haneda among others. Four islands are linked to it by bridge: Irabu, Shimoji, Kurima and Ikema. JNTO says the local bus service is not easy to use and suggests a rental car or taxi.

OCVB notes that taxis and buses are limited on remote islands, so check in advance.

## Planning notes

- On JMA's averages, typhoon approaches are rare from December to April and low in November (0.3 a year). Many resort beaches open in March (OCVB). The rainy season falls in May and June.
- July to October carries the highest typhoon risk. Build a spare day into island-hopping plans and check JMA forecasts before ferry days.
- January and February bring cherry blossom and whale season, with mean temperatures around 17°C in Naha.
- Buy Yui Rail passes just before you ride, as the validity runs from purchase.

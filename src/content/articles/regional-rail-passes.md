Each JR company sells its own passes for visitors, valid only in its own area or on set routes. For a trip that stays in one region, one of these is often much cheaper than the nationwide Japan Rail Pass. This article lists the main regional passes, with prices taken from each company's own page, and explains how to compare them.

## The national pass, for comparison

The nationwide Japan Rail Pass is sold for 7, 14 or 21 days. Bought online from the official site, an ordinary-class adult pass costs ¥50,000 for 7 days, ¥80,000 for 14 days and ¥100,000 for 21 days. Bought from an overseas agency, it costs ¥53,000, ¥84,000 and ¥105,000. Full details are in the [rail guide](/guide/rail-and-the-jr-pass), and whether it pays off is covered in [Is the JR Pass still worth it?](/questions/is-the-jr-pass-still-worth-it)

## The Kansai Area Pass has ended

JR West's page for the Kansai Area Pass (1 to 4 days, covering Osaka, Kyoto, Kobe, Nara, Himeji and the HARUKA airport express) states that sales ended on 30 September 2026. Passes bought by that date stay valid for their full period. It no longer appears in JR West's current list of passes, which includes the Kansai Mini Pass and the Kansai WIDE Area Pass.

## What regional passes do not cover

Read the coverage on the official page before you buy. Common limits:

- **Tokaido Shinkansen.** None of the JR West passes above cover the Tokaido Shinkansen between Shin-Osaka and Tokyo. The Takayama-Hokuriku pass does not cover the Tokaido Shinkansen between Kyoto and Shin-Osaka either.
- **Sanyo Shinkansen limits.** The Kansai-Hiroshima pass does not cover Hiroshima to Hakata. The Kansai WIDE and Kansai-Hokuriku passes stop at Okayama.
- **Hokkaido Shinkansen.** The Hokkaido Rail Pass does not cover it, nor the South Hokkaido Railway, Sapporo's subway or streetcars.
- **Reserved seats and Green cars.** JR West passes cover reserved ordinary seats on included Shinkansen and limited express trains, booked before boarding. Green (first class) cars need extra tickets. The Takayama-Hokuriku pass includes up to six free reserved-seat bookings; all limited express seats in Hokkaido are reserved and need a seat ticket before boarding.
- **Seasonal rules.** The Takayama-Hokuriku pass page notes that from 25 December 2026 to 5 January 2027 all seats on the Limited Express Hida (Nagoya to Takayama to Toyama) are reserved only, so a free reserved-seat ticket is needed.

## Who can buy them

The JR West and JR Kyushu pages limit their passes to holders of a foreign passport entering Japan with "Temporary Visitor" status. JR West's pages state that Japanese nationals cannot use the passes, even with permanent residency abroad. JR Hokkaido's page states that its passes are for non-Japanese passport holders, and that a valid passport must be shown (residence cards and copies are not accepted).

## How to compare

1. List each JR journey you plan, with dates.
2. Group the days spent in one region. If a block of 3 to 7 days stays within one pass area, price the regional pass for that block.
3. Add the regional pass to individual tickets for the journeys outside it (for example a Tokyo to Kyoto Shinkansen ticket at each end).
4. Compare that total with a 7-day or 14-day Japan Rail Pass at ¥50,000 or ¥80,000 (online, adult).

As a scale check: the 5-day Kansai-Hiroshima pass (¥17,000) and the 7-day Kansai-Hokuriku pass (¥19,000) each cost less than half of a 7-day national pass bought online. The national pass is more likely to be worth it only when the trip covers several JR companies' areas within its validity, including long Shinkansen legs that no single regional pass covers.

## Before you go

- Check the official page on the day you buy. The prices here are as listed on 7 November 2026 and can change.
- Note whether the pass counts consecutive days. All the passes in the table do.
- Reserve seats in advance at busy times. See [Japan's peak holiday periods](/articles/peak-holiday-periods).
- Private railways are not part of any JR pass. See the [rail guide](/guide/rail-and-the-jr-pass) and the [rail-route table](/rail-routes).

Naoshima, Teshima and Inujima sit in the Seto Inland Sea between Okayama on Honshu and Takamatsu on Shikoku. Together they hold the museums and installations of Benesse Art Site Naoshima, and every three years they are venues of the Setouchi Triennale. This article covers what is on each island, how tickets work, the ferries and the festival dates.

## Benesse Art Site Naoshima

Benesse Art Site Naoshima is, in its own words, the "collective name for all art-related activities" run by Benesse Corporation and the Fukutake Foundation. It covers Naoshima and Teshima in Kagawa Prefecture and Inujima in Okayama Prefecture. The official site also lists one facility, MECON, on Megijima.

Facilities listed on the official site in November 2026:

| Island | Facilities |
|---|---|
| Naoshima | Naoshima New Museum of Art; Chichu Art Museum; Benesse House Museum; Hiroshi Sugimoto Gallery: Time Corridors; Lee Ufan Museum; Art House Project; ANDO MUSEUM; Ring of Fire - Solar Yang and Lunar Weerasethakul; Naoshima Bath; Setouchi Archive/Miyanoura Gallery 6; The Naoshima Plan "The Water" |
| Teshima | Teshima Art Museum; Les Archives du Coeur; Teshima Yokoo House; Teshima 8 Million Lab; Needle factory; Tom Na H-iu; La foret des murmures |
| Inujima | Inujima Seirensho Art Museum; Inujima "Art House Project"; Inujima Life Garden; INUJIMA Art Rendezvous |
| Megijima | MECON |

Chichu Art Museum was designed by Tadao Ando and has works by Claude Monet, James Turrell and Walter De Maria on permanent display. Teshima Art Museum unites the work of artist Rei Naito and architect Ryue Nishizawa. The Naoshima New Museum of Art, also designed by Tadao Ando, opened in spring 2025 near the Honmura district.

## Tickets and reservations

Since 1 October 2024 the art facilities on Naoshima, Teshima and Inujima have sold tickets online. Some facilities use a reservation system that requires online purchase, and tickets for these are for a set date and time. The official ticket page lists these timed facilities:

- Chichu Art Museum
- Hiroshi Sugimoto Gallery: Time Corridors
- Art House Project "Kinza" and "Minamidera"
- Ring of Fire Lunar (night)
- Teshima Art Museum

Chichu Art Museum and Teshima Art Museum both state "Advance reservation with date and time required." Teshima Art Museum adds that if online tickets sell out, there are no on-site sales that day. The official site warns that online tickets may sell out and recommends booking before you visit. Online prices are lower than on-site prices at the museums listed below. Children aged 15 and under are free at these museums, but you still reserve for the number of people at the timed ones.

Naoshima Bath, The Naoshima Plan "The Water", La foret des murmures, Inujima Life Garden and INUJIMA Art Rendezvous are listed as needing no reservation. Children aged five and under cannot enter Minamidera or Ring of Fire. Kinza admits one person per time slot.

Closing days differ by island and by season. As stated on each museum's official page in November 2026:

Teshima Art Museum opens on national holidays and closes the following day. Benesse Art Site publishes an open-days calendar on its website. Check it for the exact dates of your visit.

## Ferries from Uno and Takamatsu

Naoshima has two main ports, Miyanoura and Honmura. The Benesse Art Site access page lists these Naoshima routes, all run by Shikoku Kisen:

- **Takamatsu to Miyanoura.** Shikoku Kisen runs a ferry (about 50 minutes outbound, about 60 minutes back) and a high-speed passenger boat (about 30 minutes). Listed one-way adult fares are ¥680 by ferry and ¥1,590 by high-speed boat.
- **Uno to Miyanoura.** Ferry about 20 minutes, passenger boat about 15 minutes. Listed one-way adult fare ¥370 on both.
- **Uno to Honmura.** About 20 minutes, according to Benesse Art Site.

Shikoku Kisen does not take boarding reservations. Seats are first come, first served. The fares above are as listed on its route pages in November 2026; the pages give no revision date.

Shikoku Kisen also runs Miyanoura to Ieura (Teshima) and Inujima. These boats depend on museum opening days and may skip a stop when that island's museum is closed.

Uno Port is about three minutes on foot from Uno Station. Benesse Art Site gives the JR Uno Line from Okayama Station as about 50 minutes. Takamatsu Port is about five minutes on foot from Takamatsu Station. For the rail link between Okayama and Takamatsu across the Seto Ohashi bridge, see [rail routes](/rail-routes).

## The Setouchi Triennale

The Setouchi Triennale is a contemporary art festival held every three years on the islands of the Seto Inland Sea, organised by the Setouchi Triennale Executive Committee. It was launched in 2010. The official archive lists editions in 2010, 2013, 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2025. The festival describes 2025 as its sixth edition.

The 2025 edition ran in three sessions, 107 days in total:

| Session | 2025 dates |
|---|---|
| Spring | 18 April to 25 May (38 days) |
| Summer | 1 August to 31 August (31 days) |
| Autumn | 3 October to 9 November (38 days) |

The next edition is in 2028. The official call for art proposals lists these sessions, 117 days in total, with venues closed one day a week in each session:

| Session | 2028 dates |
|---|---|
| Spring | 14 April to 28 May (45 days) |
| Summer | 25 July to 20 August (27 days) |
| Autumn | 29 September to 12 November (45 days) |

Venues in all three 2028 sessions are Naoshima, Teshima, Megijima, Ogijima, Shodoshima, Oshima, Inujima, the Takamatsu Port area and the Uno Port area. Some areas take part in only one session. Benesse Art Site's access page notes that ferry timetables may change during the festival.

- Check each museum's closing days against your dates.
- Ferries are first come, first served. Check the operator's timetable and same-day service status before travelling.

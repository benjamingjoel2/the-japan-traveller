The National Tax Agency states that the refund method was implemented from 1 November 2026. Any purchase made on or after that date follows the new rules. Purchases up to 31 October 2026 fell under the previous system, where eligible visitors paid no consumption tax at the till.

## At the shop

- You pay the full tax-inclusive price.
- Purchases must total ¥5,000 or more, excluding tax, per day at the same store.
- There is no set maximum. Goods are limited to what you can personally carry out of Japan when you leave.
- The old split between general goods and consumables, and the sealed packaging for consumables, have been abolished. Food, drink or cosmetics you use in Japan do not qualify for a refund.
- The shop explains how to register your refund details, such as a card or bank account.

Eligible purchasers are non-residents. The Japan Tourism Agency says it must be possible to confirm that you entered Japan less than six months ago. Japanese nationals living abroad must show specific documents, such as a valid My Number Card or a recent residence certificate.

## At departure

Within 90 days of the purchase date, and before you check in any baggage, present your passport at a tax-free procedure terminal in the international departure lobby. Have all the goods with you. A green result completes the step. A red result means customs inspects your goods.

At Narita, Haneda, Kansai, Chubu, Fukuoka, New Chitose and Naha airports, you can also start the procedure online through Visit Japan Web. That route is open to people with "Temporary Visitor", "Diplomat" or "Official" status, not to Japanese nationals.

Customs checks each receipt as a whole. If one item from a receipt is missing, nothing on that receipt is refunded. Finish the procedure before boarding: there is no compensation for a missed flight.

## Getting the money back

After customs confirms the sale, the tax-free shop or its refund service provider pays the refund. Official pages list bank transfer, credit card, app-based transfer and cash at the departure port as possible methods, depending on the shop.

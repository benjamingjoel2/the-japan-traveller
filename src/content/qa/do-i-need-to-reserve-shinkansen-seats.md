## Reserved and non-reserved cars

Shinkansen and limited express trains generally have three kinds of seat: reserved Green Car seats, reserved ordinary seats and non-reserved seats. With a non-reserved ticket you sit in any free seat in the non-reserved cars. A reservation gives you a seat ticket for a specific seat. A Nozomi normally has non-reserved seats in cars 1 and 2, according to JR Central and JR West.

JR Central states that reserved seats with an oversized baggage area can be booked from one month ahead. JR Central and JR West also state that their online services for the Tokaido, Sanyo and Kyushu Shinkansen (Express Reservation and SmartEX) take bookings for some seats up to one year ahead.

## When a reservation is required

**All-reserved trains.** JNTO names the Hayabusa (Tohoku Shinkansen) and the Kagayaki (Hokuriku Shinkansen) as trains with no non-reserved seats. Some other limited express and sightseeing trains are also all-reserved.

**Nozomi in peak periods.** JR Central and JR West run every Nozomi between Tokyo and Hakata with all seats reserved during the main holiday periods. For FY2026 the periods are:

| Period | Dates |
|---|---|
| Golden Week | 24 April to 6 May 2026 |
| Obon | 7 to 16 August 2026 |
| Silver Week | 18 to 23 September 2026 |
| Year end and New Year | 25 December 2026 to 5 January 2027 |

During these periods you cannot sit on a Nozomi with a non-reserved ticket. You may stand in the deck areas of the ordinary cars, but you may not be able to board a full train. Hikari, Kodama, Mizuho, Sakura and Tsubame trains keep their non-reserved cars, though JR Central warns that Hikari and Kodama may be very crowded.

**Oversized baggage.** On the Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and Nishi-Kyushu Shinkansen, baggage whose three dimensions total 161 to 250 cm needs a reserved "seat with an oversized baggage area". The seat costs the same as a normal reserved seat. Bags up to 160 cm need no reservation, and bags over 250 cm are not allowed. If you board with an oversized bag and no reservation, JR Central charges a 1,000 yen fee (tax included) and the bag goes where the conductor directs. Non-reserved cars have no oversized baggage areas. You can book these seats at ticket offices, at reserved-seat machines or online.

## Japan Rail Pass holders

The pass is valid in non-reserved cars on its own. Seat reservations cost nothing extra. Book them online through the official JAPAN RAIL PASS Reservation site (for passes bought there), at reserved-seat ticket machines, or at a JR ticket office. Online bookings must be collected as tickets before boarding. The oversized baggage rule applies to pass holders too.

The pass alone does not cover any seat on Nozomi or Mizuho trains. The official site sells a separate special ticket for these. See [is the JR Pass still worth it](/questions/is-the-jr-pass-still-worth-it) and the [rail guide](/guide/rail-and-the-jr-pass).

The official pass site warns that seats on particular trains may not be available during these peak periods: 1 to 6 May, 7 to 16 August (Obon), 18 to 23 September and 29 December to 3 January.

## In short

Outside the busy holidays, on a train with non-reserved cars and with a small bag, a non-reserved ticket is enough. You need a reservation on an all-reserved train, on a Nozomi in a peak period, or with a bag of 161 to 250 cm on the Tokaido, Sanyo and Kyushu lines.

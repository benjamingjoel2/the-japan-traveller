## The normal dates

Cherry blossom dates are measured on a specimen tree. Flowering is declared when five or six flowers are open; full bloom when about 80 per cent of buds are open. The Japan Weather Association lists Kyoto's normal flowering date around 26 March and full bloom around 4 April. "Normal" is the 30-year average for 1991–2020.

Trees usually look their best from full bloom until the petals start falling, roughly a week. That puts the best week, in an average year, around the end of March and the first days of April.

## Why it moves

The dates depend on the winter and early spring temperatures, so they shift every year. Plan for the normal window and treat any specific week as provisional until the forecasts come out. Forecasts are published from late winter by Japanese weather companies and updated through March.

## How to plan

- Book Kyoto accommodation for a span that covers late March and early April, rather than a single weekend.
- Different varieties and places flower at different times. Weeping cherries and trees at higher elevation (northern Kyoto, Ohara) often flower later than the city centre.
- If you miss Kyoto's peak, the blossom front continues north: the Alps and Tohoku in mid to late April. See [April on the season wheel](/seasons/april).
- This is the busiest time of year in Kyoto. Popular inns fill early: see [How far ahead should I book a ryokan?](/questions/how-far-ahead-should-i-book-a-ryokan)

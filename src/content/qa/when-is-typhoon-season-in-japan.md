## The months that matter

The Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) publishes the average number of typhoons each month, based on the 30 years from 1991 to 2020. It counts a typhoon as "approaching" when its centre comes within 300 km of a JMA weather station. It counts a landfall when the centre reaches the coastline of Hokkaido, Honshu, Shikoku or Kyushu.

| Month | Approaches to Okinawa and Amami | Approaches to the main islands | Landfalls |
|---|---|---|---|
| May | 0.4 | 0.2 | 0.0 |
| June | 0.6 | 0.4 | 0.2 |
| July | 1.5 | 1.0 | 0.6 |
| August | 2.3 | 1.6 | 0.9 |
| September | 2.0 | 1.9 | 1.0 |
| October | 1.1 | 0.9 | 0.3 |
| November | 0.3 | none recorded | none recorded |
| Year | 7.9 | 5.8 | 3.0 |

From December to April, approaches are close to zero on these averages. JMA's figures also show an average of 25.1 typhoons forming each year, with the most forming in August (5.7) and September (5.0).

## Okinawa and the main islands

- **Okinawa and Amami** see the most approaches: 7.9 a year on average, against 5.8 for the main islands. August is the peak month, followed by September and July.
- **The main islands** (Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu and Shikoku) see the most approaches in September, followed by August. Landfalls are also most frequent in September.
- **October** still averages around one approach to both areas.

JNTO's weather page warns that August and September may bring typhoons that can disrupt travel plans. For Okinawa in particular, see [Okinawa: when to go and how to get around](/articles/okinawa-when-to-go).

## What it means for travel

These are averages, not forecasts. A typhoon can arrive in a quieter month or miss a busy one. JNTO notes that typhoons can upset travel plans, and the Okinawa Convention and Visitors Bureau notes that weather can affect inter-island ferries.

- **Watch JMA.** JMA's English site has a heavy rain and typhoon section, warnings and risk maps. JMA also provides weather, heavy rain, earthquake, tsunami and volcano information in 15 languages.
- **Install the Safety tips app.** The Japan Tourism Agency's Safety tips app pushes earthquake early warnings, tsunami warnings and other weather warnings within Japan, in 15 languages.
- **Keep plans flexible.** On islands and in places reached by ferry, leave a spare day before an onward flight.
- **Typhoons follow the rainy season.** On the main islands the rainy season usually ends in July; see [Is Japan good in the rainy season?](/questions/is-japan-good-in-the-rainy-season) and the [seasons guide](/guide/seasons).

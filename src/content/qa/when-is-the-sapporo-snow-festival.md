## Dates for 2027

The festival's official site lists the 2027 Sapporo Snow Festival from Thursday 4 February to Thursday 11 February 2027 (11 February is a public holiday). All three sites are open on the same dates:

| Site | Where | 2027 dates |
|---|---|---|
| Odori Site | Odori Park, central Sapporo | 4–11 February |
| Tsudome Site | Sapporo Community Dome (Tsudome), Higashi-ku | 4–11 February |
| Susukino Site | Main street in Susukino, Minami 4-jo to Minami 7-jo | 4–11 February |

As of 7 November 2026, the official site said further information on the 2027 festival would be added over time. It had not yet published opening hours, and the Sapporo Tourist Association's page did not give them either. Check the official site nearer the date.

## Usual timing

The festival is held in early to mid February, the timing JR Hokkaido gives on its winter route page. The 2025 edition, the 75th, also ran from 4 to 11 February at the same three sites.

According to the Sapporo Tourist Association, the festival began in 1950 with six snow sculptures made by local junior and high school students. It now draws more than two million visitors a year.

## What each site offers

The Sapporo Tourist Association describes the three sites:

- **Odori.** The main site. Snow sculptures line a 1.5 km stretch of the park from 1-chome to 12-chome and are lit from sundown to 10pm. The International Snow Sculpture Contest takes place at 11-chome.
- **Tsudome.** A giant slide and indoor and outdoor snow activities.
- **Susukino.** An ice sculpture contest, illuminated ice sculptures and an ice bar.

## At the same time

The Otaru Snow Light Path runs from 6 to 13 February 2027, according to the Sapporo Tourist Association. Its venues include the Otaru Canal and the former Temiya railway line. JR Hokkaido's winter route page also lists the Sapporo White Illumination, which runs from late November to mid March.

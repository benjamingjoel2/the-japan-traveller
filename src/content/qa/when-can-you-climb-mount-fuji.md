## The 2026 season by trail

| Trail | Opened | Closed |
|---|---|---|
| Yoshida (Yamanashi) | 1 July | 10 September |
| Subashiri (Shizuoka) | 1 July | 10 September |
| Fujinomiya (Shizuoka) | 10 July | 10 September |
| Gotemba (Shizuoka) | 10 July | 10 September |

Opening can be delayed by weather or by snow still on the trail. The 2026 season has ended. For the next season the official climbing website gives "early July to early September", with exact dates to be announced, so check fujisan-climb.jp before you plan.

Early July overlaps the rainy season across much of Japan; see [Is Japan good in the rainy season?](/questions/is-japan-good-in-the-rainy-season). The official site also warns that sudden weather changes and thunderstorms are not uncommon in summer.

## Outside the season

All trails to the summit are closed outside the season. According to the official climbing website:

- Use of the trail from the 5th station to the summit is prohibited under the Road Act. Penalties are up to six months' imprisonment or a fine of up to 300,000 yen.
- The Guidelines for Ensuring Safety During Mount Fuji Climbs (revised March 2021) prohibit climbing outside the summer season for anyone not fully prepared. Those who do climb must submit a climbing plan and carry a portable toilet.
- From November to April the summit's average daily minimum is below -10 degrees C. Huts, toilets and the first-aid centre are closed, mobile reception is almost nonexistent and rescue may be difficult.

## Fees, registration and bookings

In 2026 every trail had a hiking fee of 4,000 yen per person.

- **Yoshida Trail:** the 5th station gate is closed to climbers from 14:00 to 3:00 the next day, and closes once 4,000 climbers have entered that day. Mountain hut guests are exempt from both. Passage reservations are optional and can be made online up to the day of the climb.
- **Shizuoka trails:** register in the "Shizuoka Prefecture FUJI NAVI" app, which opened for 2026 on 8 May. You complete e-learning, pass a test, pay the fee and receive a QR-code permit. There is no daily cap, but entering between 14:00 and 3:00 requires a mountain hut stay. On-site registration at the 5th station is possible and takes about 30 minutes.
- **Mountain huts** are booked directly with each hut, not through the app or the Yoshida reservation system.

For the four trails, rules, safety advice and access to the 5th stations, see [Climbing Mount Fuji: season, trails and the new rules](/articles/climbing-mount-fuji).

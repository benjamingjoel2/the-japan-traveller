There is no single booking window that applies to every ryokan. Each inn sets its own, and many have only a handful of rooms. What follows is how to judge it.

## Dates that fill first

- **Cherry blossom**: late March to early April in Tokyo and Kyoto; later further north.
- **Autumn leaves in Kyoto**: mid November to early December.
- **Golden Week**: 29 April to 5 May.
- **Obon**: mid August.
- **New Year**: roughly 29 December to 3 January.
- **Weekends and Saturday nights** in onsen towns near big cities, such as Hakone, all year.

For these dates, book as soon as the inn you want opens reservations.

## Off-peak

Outside those periods, many ryokan have rooms a few weeks out, particularly midweek and away from Kyoto and Hakone. Small, well-known inns are the exception and can be full at any time of year.

## What to arrange when you book

- **Meals**: whether dinner is included, and the dinner time.
- **Dietary needs and allergies**: tell the inn at booking; kitchens plan ahead and many dishes use fish stock.
- **Private bath**: if you have tattoos or prefer to bathe privately, book a room with its own bath or ask about a private bath for hire. See [ryokan and onsen basics](/guide/ryokan-and-onsen).
- **Check-in time**: arriving late can mean missing dinner.
- **Cancellation terms**: these vary by inn and booking site; read them before you pay.

## When it is

The rainy season (tsuyu) moves north. Okinawa's usually starts in May and ends in late June. From Kyushu to Kanto (including Kyoto and Tokyo) it usually starts in early June and ends in mid or late July. Tohoku's starts slightly later. Hokkaido has no formal rainy season. The Japan Meteorological Agency announces the start and end for each region each year.

## What it is like

- Rain is frequent but it rarely rains all day every day. Many days are overcast with showers.
- It is warm and humid, more so in July.
- Gardens and moss temples are at their greenest, and hydrangeas flower in June (Kamakura and Kyoto have well-known hydrangea temples).
- Spells of heavy rain can delay trains and close mountain roads and trails. Check warnings before hiking.
- June has no national holiday, so domestic travel is quieter than in spring or August.

## Where to go instead

- **Hokkaido**: early summer, no formal rainy season.
- **Okinawa from July**: after its rainy season ends, though typhoon season follows.
- **Indoors and covered**: Tokyo and Osaka work well in any weather.
